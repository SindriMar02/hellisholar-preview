Stay under Eyjafjallajökull

A hotel, 24 cottages and an all-year campsite on one farm in Fljótshlíð, with a restaurant and a nine-hole golf course down to the river Þverá.

Book a stay See the stays

A farm since 1952

Hellishólar was built as a farm in 1952. Guests have stayed here since 2001, and Víðir Jóhannsson has run the place since late 2004.

Everything on one farm

The hotel, cottages and campsite sit together on the farm. The golf course runs down to Þverá. Hvolsvöllur is about 10 minutes away by car, Seljalandsfoss 25 km.

Map of the grounds

Golf on the banks of Þverá

Þverárvöllur runs along Þverá and Grjótá and is run by Golfklúbburinn Þverá. Club and cart hire, a driving range and a putting green.

The golf course

Hygge restaurant and bar

Seats for 130, food made from scratch

A regular restaurant through the summer, 07:00 to 23:00. Mostly gluten free and vegan friendly. Birthdays, weddings and meetings the rest of the year.

The restaurant

Summer and winter

The campsite is open all year and needs no booking. Rooms and cottages book online.

Groups, schools and events

School groups have come here since 2004. The hall seats 130 with a large screen and a projector, and groups sleep in the hotel and the cottages.
