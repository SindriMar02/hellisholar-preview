Room for the whole group

313 beds, full meals and a hall for 130 on one farm.

Groups, schools and events

We have hosted school groups since 2004, between 100 and 200 groups a year, and many come back every year. Every group gets breakfast, lunch and a three-course dinner, beds in the hotel, guesthouses, cottages and hostel, and a playground, karaoke, golf course and fishing lake.

The hall seats 130 with a large screen and a projector. Stay over in the hotel and the cottages, with meals from our own kitchen.

Birthdays, confirmations, weddings and family reunions. The hall is rented with catering from the kitchen.

Rooms or cottages plus rounds on Þverárvöllur. Ask for a group offer.
